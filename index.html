About Inner Himalaya

Pioneering Journeys Across the Highest Horizons

Inner Himalaya Trek & Tour is a licensed trekking agency nestled in the foothills of Nepal. We specialize in bespoke private expeditions, classic tea-house routes, and remote mountaineering adventures suited for all levels of mountain lovers.

Safety-First Logistics

High-altitude medical kits, satellite comms, and wilderness-first-aid certified team leaders.

Responsible & Sustainable Tourism

We champion zero-trace trails, fair porter welfare standards, and invest 10% of company profits into remote education NGOs.

Impact Driven

Every booking directly funds seasonal school gear for children across Solukhumbu and Manang.
